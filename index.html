Monthly take-home income is used to calculate proportional splits — the partner who earns more covers a proportionally larger share.

Data

Data is saved locally in this browser only. It won't sync to other devices or browsers, and clearing your browser data will erase it.

Expenses are logged individually with their own charge date — nothing repeats automatically, so log each one (past, today, or upcoming) as it comes up. Update income and your grocery/food budget in Setup whenever they change.
